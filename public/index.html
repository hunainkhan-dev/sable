FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does implementation take?

Most teams are live within a week. Sable auto-maps your chart of accounts and connects to your ERP, banks, and cards with native integrations.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Sable is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR-ready, with encryption at rest and in transit, granular access controls, and a full audit trail on every action.

Does the AI make changes on its own?

Never without approval. Sable drafts entries and flags anomalies, but a human on your team reviews and approves everything before it’s posted.

Which systems do you integrate with?

NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, Brex, Ramp, Plaid, Gusto, and more — with a REST API for anything custom.

Can we cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month with no lock-in, and you can export all of your data at any time.
